ABOUT ME

THE PERSON BEHIND EK-JOURNEY

Hello, I'm Erik Pan

Independent Game Developer | Unity & C#

I am an independent game developer passionate about creating immersive worlds, engaging gameplay and memorable experiences.

I completed a two-year game development course with Unity at Deusto Formación and earned the Unity Certified User: Programmer certification. I also expanded my knowledge through additional courses at Frogames and completed a 300-hour internship at Maedcore, gaining practical experience with virtual reality and 3D gameplay mechanics.

My portfolio includes projects such as Ethereal Ascent, Space Call and John Lemon's Haunted Jaunt. I also completed web development training through SEF, learning HTML, CSS and JavaScript, which I used to build EK-Journey myself.

I am currently working on new game projects while continuing to improve my programming skills and explore new areas of game development.

EK-Journey represents years of hard work, learning, determination and dreams. It is a place where I share my projects, progress and experiences as I continue growing as a developer.

Thank you for visiting. I hope you enjoy exploring my journey.
