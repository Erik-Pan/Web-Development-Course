SYNOPSIS

Henry Blood is a man devastated by the tragic loss of his wife. Unable to accept her death, he performs a mysterious ritual in the desperate hope of bringing her back. However, the ritual transports him into a supernatural world filled with darkness, mystery and unimaginable dangers.

Trapped inside a sinister labyrinth, Henry must explore its corridors, uncover hidden secrets and survive encounters with terrifying creatures lurking in the shadows. Driven by love, grief and the hope of seeing his wife again, he must find a way to complete his journey through this supernatural nightmare. But what dangers await him along the way?
